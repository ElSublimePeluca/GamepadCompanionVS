GamepadCompanion — descripción para la página de ModDB
(en inglés, que es el idioma de la página; el texto va tal cual)

================================================================================

Play Vintage Story with a controller.

Vintage Story has no gamepad support of its own. This mod adds it: movement and
camera on the sticks, a radial menu for the things a controller has no buttons
left for, a cursor you can drive around inventories, and — since 1.12.0 — the
game's own key hints showing your controller's buttons instead of keyboard keys.

It is a client mod. Nothing is required on the server, and you can join any
server with it.


WHAT IT DOES

The game's hints speak your controller
  Look at a chest and the help sign says "[LT]: Open" instead of naming a mouse
  button. The same goes for the hint on the item in your hand, the survival
  handbook and item tooltips. Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo icon sets, picked
  automatically from the controller you plugged in, or chosen by hand. On
  PlayStation the four face buttons are drawn as the real symbols.

Movement and camera
  Sticks, with separate horizontal and vertical sensitivity, an adjustable dead
  zone and optional pitch inversion. A precision mode on D-pad up divides the
  camera speed for aiming at a specific block.

A radial menu
  Hold LB and pick with the right stick. Twelve slots, all configurable:
  hotkeys, dialogs, single key presses, or several actions in sequence.

A cursor for inventories
  When a dialog is open a cursor appears. The D-pad jumps it slot by slot, which
  is what you want most of the time; hold RB and the stick moves it freely. The
  triggers click.

Sneak and sprint as toggles
  L3 and R3 turn them on and off rather than being held, with an indicator in
  the corner. They are the game's Shift and Ctrl, so they double as the modifier
  for clicks: stacking, placing, and anything else a mod hangs off them.

Hold a key while a button is held
  Some mods read a modifier key at the moment of the click. A normal key binding
  sends the press and release in the same frame and misses that window, so there
  is a separate binding type that keeps the key down for as long as you hold the
  button.

An on-screen keyboard
  For chat and commands, so you can play without reaching for the keyboard.

An in-game editor
  Everything above is configured from a dialog, on the Insert key by default or
  with .gpconfig. Settings persist to a JSON file.


REQUIREMENTS

Vintage Story 1.22 or later. Any controller the system exposes as a joystick.
Tested on Linux and Windows with Xbox, DualShock/DualSense and GameSir pads,
over USB and Bluetooth.


IF YOUR CONTROLLER IS NOT PICKED UP

Some devices that are not gamepads present themselves as joysticks — RGB
controllers on motherboards, keyboards with programmable keys. Run .gpdevice to
see every joystick the game can see, which one is in use and why the others were
skipped, and .gpdevice <number> to force the right one.

All the mod's commands are client commands: they take a dot, not a slash.


A NOTE ON HOW IT WORKS

The mod writes synthetic input into the client — mouse buttons, keys, camera
movement — through the game's own input pipeline, so the rest of the game and
other mods see it exactly as they would see a real keyboard and mouse. That is
what makes it work with mods it knows nothing about. It sends nothing over the
network, touches no file outside its own settings, and has no unsafe code, but
it is honest to say how it works before you install it.
