GamepadCompanion v1.13.0

Menus: the D-pad goes to the next slot or button, and the cursor no longer needs RB

  Reported by nolepguy (#9). In the inventory the crafted knife could not be
  taken out of the output slot, and flint knapping would not let the hoe be
  picked: the cursor always jumped past them.

  Until now the D-pad moved the cursor a fixed 52 pixels per press, without
  looking at what was on screen, and that distance was never tied to the layout
  or to the interface scale. The crafting output sits a bit further below the
  grid than one slot, so the cursor could stop just above it and just below it
  and never on it, however many times you pressed. In the knapping recipe
  picker each press drifted by a pixel, and depending on where the cursor
  started, a recipe could be skipped. At an interface scale other than 100% the
  steps did not even follow the inventory grid.

  Now the D-pad jumps to the nearest thing you can click in the direction you
  press: inventory and container slots, the hotbar while a menu is open, the
  recipe picker for knapping, clay forming and smithing, the tool mode
  selector, tabs, buttons, switches, drop-down lists and text fields. If there
  is nothing in that direction, the cursor stays where it is. Sliders and
  scroll bars are left out on purpose, since clicking the middle of a slider
  changes its value, and so are title bars. Anything the D-pad cannot reach,
  such as a mod window drawn with its own elements, is one push of the right
  stick away. The world map keeps its own controls: D-pad up and down zoom, and
  left and right move the cursor in steps, now scaled with the interface.

  The right stick now moves the cursor in any menu on its own. Holding RB is no
  longer needed, and RB does nothing in menus. The stick uses the dead zone from
  the Sensitivity tab, the same one as the camera. To take the cursor back from
  a physical mouse it has to be pushed deliberately, so a controller with a bit
  of drift resting on the desk does not fight your mouse.

New: A clicks in menus

  With a menu open, A clicks just like RT. The two count as a single button, so
  pressing both, in any order, never clicks twice. A is meant for the D-pad:
  your left thumb moves between slots and your right thumb confirms. RT remains
  the natural click when you point with the right stick, because your thumb and
  index finger can move the cursor and hold the click at the same time, which
  is what dragging items across slots needs.

  If you assigned something to A in the Buttons tab, A keeps doing that, and RT
  is the click.

Nothing changed outside menus

  Movement, the camera, the triggers in the world, jumping with A, and what the
  D-pad does while you play (hotbar, precision mode, sitting) are exactly as
  they were in 1.12.0.
