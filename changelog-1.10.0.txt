GamepadCompanion v1.10.0

Fixes

- The character no longer walks and spins on its own when something that is not
  a gamepad is plugged in. Reported by Lyn_ on SteamOS: stuck turning in
  circles while looking up and to the left, with the controller itself ignored
  — sometimes it was picked up after a relaunch, sometimes not.

  The mod took the first joystick the system offered it, and the system does
  not only call gamepads joysticks. On that machine the first one was the
  motherboard's RGB lighting controller, which reports twelve buttons and ten
  axes — gamepad-shaped enough to be chosen — and holds every one of those axes
  at its extreme forever. Read as a gamepad, that is both sticks pushed hard
  into a corner and held there: walk forward-left, camera up-left, no way to
  stop it. The real controller was never even looked at, which is why input
  came and went with whatever order the system happened to enumerate devices
  in.

  Devices are now checked before being adopted: a resting gamepad has its
  sticks near centre, so one that starts with them pinned at the extreme is
  skipped and the scan moves on. The check repeats every frame while no gamepad
  is connected, so a real controller that was being held at the time is taken
  as soon as it is let go, and a device that only misbehaves after connecting
  is dropped within a couple of seconds.

New

- `.gpdevice` lists every joystick the game can see, says which one is in use
  and why the others were skipped. `.gpdevice <number>` forces a specific one
  and remembers it for future sessions; `.gpdevice auto` goes back to
  autodetection. No heuristic can recognise every gamepad-shaped device that
  exists, so when the guess is wrong there is now a way to say so.

- The client log records all joysticks present at the moment one is adopted,
  not just the one that was chosen. A bug report is now enough on its own to
  tell whether the right device was picked.
