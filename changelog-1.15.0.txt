GamepadCompanion v1.15.0

Menus: the D-pad only moves the cursor, even if you assigned something to it

  Reported by pngwn: with D-pad down set to hold Z for the ZoomButton mod,
  moving down through the inventory zoomed the camera in.

  With a menu open, the D-pad moves the cursor from slot to slot. Its
  defaults already stood aside there, but anything you had assigned to it
  yourself kept running on top of the cursor move, so both happened at once.
  There is no assignment for which that is what you want, so with a menu
  open the D-pad now only moves the cursor, whatever you put on it. Outside
  menus nothing changes.

  If a D-pad button was already holding a key when the menu opened, the key
  stays down until you let go of the button, like a keyboard key you keep
  pressed while opening your inventory.

New: the radial menu opens over menus on the new layout

  Also pngwn's: with the inventory moved into the wheel to free up Y, there
  was no way to open a chest and your inventory at the same time. On the new
  layout the wheel lives on D-pad up, and with a menu open D-pad up moves the
  cursor, so the wheel simply did not open there. The same thing got in the
  way of chat: an open chat box counts as a menu, so after picking Chat from
  the wheel you could not get back to it for the virtual keyboard.

  Now, with a menu open, a tap of D-pad up still moves the cursor, and
  holding it for 0.4 seconds opens the wheel (the press itself still moves
  the cursor up one place first). Outside menus the wheel opens as soon as
  you press the button, as before. While the virtual keyboard is up the
  D-pad belongs to the keyboard, and the wheel stays closed. The classic
  layout keeps the wheel on LB, which never had this conflict, so nothing
  changes there.

Fixes

- Cancelling the wheel with B also dropped the item in your hand, or closed
  the menu underneath it. The wheel closed on that press, but the same press
  still reached B's own action, which is to drop the item or close the menu.
  It had been there since the very first version, and now that the wheel
  opens over menus it would have closed your chest every time you cancelled.

- On the new layout, the game's hints showed LB for anything you put in the
  wheel — the key that opens the handbook came out as [LB], for example —
  and on that layout LB moves through the hotbar. They now show the button
  that really opens the wheel: D-pad up on the new layout, LB on the classic
  one.
