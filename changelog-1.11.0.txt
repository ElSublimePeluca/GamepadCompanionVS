GamepadCompanion v1.11.0

Fixes

- Controllers that identify themselves as a DualShock are mapped correctly
  again. Reported with a GameSir Cyclone 2 in PS4 mode over Bluetooth: the
  central buttons no longer paused or opened the map, one of them toggled a
  modifier instead, and the triggers fired pause and map on top of their own
  job.

  In PS4 mode a controller has to present the console with Sony's own vendor
  and product id, or the console refuses it. Over USB the manufacturer string
  survives and the mod recognised the pad by name; over Bluetooth nothing is
  left but "Wireless Controller" and a DualShock 4 id, so the mod fell back to
  the layout it assumes when it knows nothing — the classic Linux xpad one.

  Those two layouts differ by exactly two buttons. The Sony driver exposes L2
  and R2 a second time as plain digital buttons, which xpad does not have, and
  that pair pushes everything after it two places along: Share, Options, PS and
  the two stick clicks land where xpad puts Back, Start, Guide and the stick
  clicks. Read with the wrong layout, squeezing a trigger also pressed the
  button that opens the map or pauses the game, Options toggled Ctrl, the PS
  button toggled Shift, clicking the sticks did nothing at all, and X and Y
  were swapped.

  Recognition no longer depends on the name. A pad is now read as a DualShock
  when it reports Sony's vendor id, or when it has the shape of one: six axes
  with both triggers resting at -1, and thirteen real buttons where xpad has
  eleven. This covers actual DualShock 3, 4 and DualSense pads on Linux as well
  as anything wearing their id. Controllers that were already working are
  untouched — an Xbox pad on xpad reports eleven buttons and still takes the
  xpad path.

  When a controller does end up on the fallback, the log line now carries its
  guid, its hat count and its real button count, which is what was missing to
  diagnose this in one pass instead of two.

- The Ctrl, Shift and precision indicators no longer sit on top of the minimap.
  Reported by nolepguy (#6). Both were pinned to the top right corner. When the
  minimap is in that corner the indicators now tuck in just underneath it, with
  their right edge lined up against its own; if the minimap is somewhere else,
  or turned off, they stay where they always were. Opening the full-screen map
  does not make them jump, and they follow the minimap if you move it.

- Dialog panels are no longer narrower than their own title bar. Reported by
  nolepguy (#7).

  Every dialog in the mod declared a size that already included its margins,
  and then asked the background to shrink-wrap itself around its contents. The
  background obliged, which quietly ate the right and bottom margin, while the
  title bar kept the declared width — so the bar overhung the panel by sixteen
  pixels on the right, the footer button sat flush against the bottom edge, and
  in the virtual keyboard the title sat off-centre. The backgrounds now use the
  size they declare. The title bar height also follows the game's own constant
  instead of a hardcoded value one pixel short of it.

- Long action names no longer spill off the settings panel. A button in Vintage
  Story grows itself so its label fits on one line, and nothing clips it
  afterwards, so a wheel slot bound to a composite of several actions painted
  its right half over the world outside the dialog. Labels are now measured and
  trimmed to the space available before they are handed over. Affects the wheel
  and gamepad button tabs, the composite builder, and the action picker list.

- The key capture dialog grows to fit its prompt. The prompt wraps to two lines
  in Spanish, and in "hold key" mode in English as well, but the dialog was a
  fixed height — so the second line was drawn underneath the Cancel button. The
  height is now measured from the text.
